SMART WEARABLE TECHNOLOGY

A small device for instant safety.

The proposed Women Safety wearable is designed to provide a simple and quick way to send an emergency alert. The device can communicate with a smartphone and the safety platform.

DEVICE CONTROL

How do you control the wearable?

The device is designed to keep emergency interaction simple. Most normal monitoring functions can be handled through the connected smartphone.

WS-001
● SYSTEM READY
01

Power ON

Turn on the wearable device. The status indicator shows that the device is ready.

02

Connect to Phone

The smartphone detects the wearable and establishes a Bluetooth connection.

03

Press SOS

When an emergency alert is required, the SOS button sends the event to the connected phone.

04

Alert Processing

The phone can forward the alert and available location information to the backend system.

EMERGENCY PROCESS

What happens when SOS is pressed?

The following represents the proposed communication flow for the safety system.

01

SOS Button Pressed

The wearable generates an emergency event.

02

Phone Receives Alert

The connected smartphone receives the wearable event.

03

Location Information

Available phone location information can be associated with the alert.

04

Backend Receives Alert

The backend API receives and stores the emergency event.

05

Dashboard Updates

The safety dashboard can display the emergency alert and status.
